## 12. Clinical Evidence Bibliography

33-entry bibliography across 11 modality sections available: `np_bib_001.docx`

**Evidence summary:**
- TMS for depression: strongest — FDA pivotal trial (n=301), CPT reimbursement codes, 50–55% response rate
- EMDR for PTSD: strong — WHO recommendation, multiple meta-analyses
- EEG neurofeedback for ADHD: strong — 21 RCTs, n=1,261
- Transcranial PBM: good but concentrated in Vielight/BU/Toronto group — Rashidi-Ranjbar 2025 RCT (n=20, multimodal neuroimaging) is strongest
- taVNS: good — scoping review 109 studies (n=3,231), largest epilepsy RCT (n=150)
- 40Hz gamma: foundational animal study (Nature 2016) + Phase 2A human RCT (n=15 AD). **The pivotal trial missed its primary endpoint:** Cognito's HOPE Phase 3 (Spectris, 40 Hz light + sound, n=673 randomised, 663 analysed, biomarker-confirmed mild-to-moderate AD, 12 months, sham-controlled) showed no difference from sham at month 12 on iADMARS, a combined MMSE + ADCS-ADL score. A prespecified mild subgroup (MMSE 21–28, n=395) favoured the device on all five measures (ADCS-ADL 56% and CDR-SB 47% slower decline; composite nominal p=.04), but those figures are not adjusted for multiplicity and only point to a next trial. Cognito's 82% figure is a per-protocol group of 169 high-adherence users and cannot show causation. Seizures: 0.9% active vs 0.3% sham (`NP-FW-HUB-001` `OI-FWHUB-37`). Source: Cognito at CTAD, 2026-11-16, as reported by NeurologyLive, Medscape and Fierce Biotech; not yet checked against a peer-reviewed publication. **No human RCT of 40 Hz tactile stimulation exists** (`NP-ACC-PRIORITY-001` `OI-ACC-10`)
- **Critical gap:** No RCT of combined multi-modal protocol (PBM + BES + VNS + audio + visual simultaneously) exists anywhere
