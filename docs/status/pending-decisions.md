### 13.2c clinical-03 dose-fidelity gap (raised 2026-07-20, DECIDED 2026-07-27)

Resolving the clinical-03 lateralization question required pinning down what `docs/pbm_neuro_protocols.md` §3 means by "60 J/cm² **per site**". The doc's own arithmetic settles it, and the answer is larger than the lateralization issue that prompted the work.

**The evidence "site" is a single ~13.6 cm² aperture.** §3 states "Dose: 60 J/cm² per site (≈816–1632 J total over 1–2 sites)". 816 J ÷ 60 J/cm² = **13.6 cm²** per site — the aperture of the laser used across the Gonzalez-Lima / Barrett lineage. So "60 J/cm²/site" is **per-site-on-target**, an areal energy density over that one spot, and "site" means one ~4 cm-diameter spot over right lateral PFC (Fp2/F4), not a region.

**One hex module is one site.** A 40 mm flat-to-flat hexagon has area (√3/2)·W² = **13.86 cm²** (NP-HEX-ZM-001 §3 design point). The evidence aperture and one NeurOne module are the same size to within 2%. That is a coincidence worth knowing, and it makes the mismatch easy to state:

| Zone | Modules | Area | vs one evidence site |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence protocol | — | 13.6 cm² (1 site) or 27.2 cm² (2 sites) | 1× |
| `Frontal Right` (as originally shipped) | 11 | 152 cm² | **11.2×** |
| `Frontal Right (excl. midline)` (as now shipped) | 8 | 111 cm² | **8.2×** |

Narrowing the zone fixed lateralization; it did **not** fix this. clinical-03 still illuminates roughly eight times the evidence footprint.

**Two further fidelity gaps in the same protocol, resolved below:**

1. **Irradiance — RESOLVED 2026-07-27 (honest relabel, not a firmware change).** The evidence is 0.25 W/cm² **CW**. NP-BIB-1064-001 records NeurOne 1064nm running pulsed at 25% duty → **0.10 W/cm² average**, 2.5× below the gold standard. The protocol file declared `frequency: 0Hz` (CW) *and* `duty_cycle: 25%` simultaneously, self-contradictory as authored. Investigation found this is not just an authoring slip: `duty_cycle: 25%` is the **firmware-enforced ceiling** for the 1064nm channel (`np_pbm1064_drive_set_duty()`, NP-FW-PBM1064-001 §5 — "cannot be bypassed by the session layer"), and the npps schema itself only accepts `duty_cycle: 1–25`; true CW (100% duty) is not an option the protocol file can request. Separately, firmware caps this channel at **36 J/cm²/session** (OI-SES-01), below the literature's 60 J/cm²/site regardless of duty. **Given the choice between (a) relabeling the protocol to honestly describe what firmware actually delivers within existing safety ceilings, or (b) raising the firmware duty/dose ceilings to actually hit the gold standard — the owner chose (a).** Raising firmware safety ceilings is out of scope for a documentation pass and was explicitly declined; it would require its own safety/regulatory review (RISK-03, FAI-SM re-test, OI-SES-01 re-confirmation). `protocols/predefined/clinical-03-pbm-cognitive-1064.npps` (Rev 1.1) now states both the duty shortfall (2.5×) and the dose ceiling (36 vs. 60 J/cm²) explicitly, and withholds the "gold-standard"/CW claim rather than asserting parameters the hardware cannot produce.
2. **A doc inconsistency at the source protocol database, still open.** §3 of `docs/pbm_neuro_protocols.md` states "Duration: 8 min (240 s/site × 2 sites, **or 480 s single site**)", which is internally inconsistent: 480 s at 0.25 W/cm² is 120 J/cm², double the stated 60 J/cm²/site. The two-site reading is the self-consistent one. Left unedited deliberately — this is a clinical evidence bibliography document (not NeurOne's own delivered-dose claim, which is addressed in item 1 above) and the correction should be made by whoever owns that document.

**Decision (2026-07-27) — option (iii): gate the evidence-grade claim behind REG-1.** Narrowing clinical-03 to the 1–2 modules the evidence actually describes means naming the modules over Fp2/F4 — which depends on gate **REG-1** (socket lattice registers to 10-20 within tolerance, NP-HEX-ZM-001 §7), still open. Until REG-1 closes there is no honest way to author an Fp2/F4-precise zone. Rather than (i) hold clinical-03 at 8 modules and accept an ~8× area deviation from a protocol carrying a **Grade A / "gold-standard parameter set"** description, or (ii) reduce module count now on the provisional lattice and accept placement uncertainty, the decision is: **withhold the evidence-grade / "Grade A" / gold-standard characterization from `clinical-03-pbm-cognitive-1064.npps` until REG-1 lands and the zone is re-authored to the true 1–2 module Fp2/F4 footprint.** This is the conservative reading of the existing marketing-claims gate posture (cf. RISK-03 / NP-REG-PBM1064-001 §7). **Important:** REG-1 landing alone does not automatically lift the gate — the duty/dose shortfall in item 1 above is independent of zone geometry and must be separately re-evaluated (or explicitly accepted as a permanent, disclosed limitation) before any "gold-standard" claim is reinstated. Protocol file updated (`protocols/predefined/clinical-03-pbm-cognitive-1064.npps` Rev 1.1); decision logged in `docs/status/completed-decisions.md`. REG-1 (NP-HEX-ZM-001 §7) updated to list this claim gate as a consumer.
