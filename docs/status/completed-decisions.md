- **Safety-MCU cranial PBM enable collapsed to one bit — firmware now matches NP-HW-HUB-001 Rev 3 §7.2 (NEW 2026-08-05)** — §7.2 decided at Rev 3 that `NP_SAFETY_EN_PBM_ZONE_0..4` becomes a single `NP_SAFETY_EN_PBM_CRANIAL` bit; the firmware never followed, so five per-zone bits survived two revisions in both `np_safety_protocol.h` and `np_hub_config.h`. This was doubly wrong: five was the retired slot count, and *zone* is a noun firmware must not model at all — a zone is a human-authored socket set in `protocols/predefined/00-zones.npps`, and under the §4.5.1 discriminator firmware may hold a socket grouping only if changing its membership requires re-tooling hardware. Because these symbols gate PBM and tES elements, a stale grouping is a **wrong-site stimulation path**, not a data-quality problem. Implemented: bit 0 is `NP_SAFETY_EN_PBM_CRANIAL`, bits 1–4 are reserved holes **excluded** from `NP_SAFETY_EN_ALL_MASK` (`0x3FFF` → `0x3FE1`), so a hub that still sets a retired zone bit has it stripped in `np_spi_watchdog_tick` rather than enabling something else; `NP_EN_PBM_ZONE0..4_PORT/PIN` collapse to `NP_EN_PBM_CRANIAL_PORT/PIN` on PA0. **The word was deliberately NOT compacted.** §7.2 justified the reservation solely by SHDR fault records, and no such records exist yet (principal, 2026-08-04), so that rationale does not currently bind — but a second, previously unstated one does: enable-bit position ≡ `current_ua[]` slot ≡ charge-accumulator index is a three-way Class C identity that the 40 µC/cm² limit rests on. §7.2 has been amended to record it (was OI-HEXTILE-14). **Latent defect found and fixed in passing:** `np_thermal_interlock.c` used the NTC channel index as an enable-bit position (`granted_mask &= ~(1U << ch)`), so after the collapse an over-temp on cranial sense domains 1–4 would have cleared reserved bits and cut **nothing** while reporting a thermal cutoff. Every cranial domain now clears the one cranial bit. Also clears the **PA4 double-assignment** (SPI1 NSS vs `NP_EN_PBM_ZONE4_PIN`, OI-HEXTILE-13 note (c) / FMEA-M08-04). - **ACC reclassified as a DEEP (indirect-modulation) HD-tDCS target (NEW 2026-08-05)** — FAI HD02-A required all 7 clinical targets within 35 mm of their nearest cap electrode. ACC (0, 28, 28) is 47.1 mm from Fz and had been failing since the suite was written. It is not a mapping bug: ACC sits on the medial wall, its 2nd/3rd nearest electrodes are 49.1 mm, and no 10-10 scalp position gets closer than ~37.9 mm (Fpz) — the criterion is unsatisfiable for ACC by **any** electrode placement. Root cause was a conflation in `np_hd_clinical_target_t` between *sLORETA source-localization* targets (deep sources localize fine) and *4×1 stimulation* targets (§2.3 claims ~1.5 cm FWHM at 10 mm depth). Resolution: targets now carry `NP_HD_TARGET_DEPTH_SURFACE`/`_DEEP` (`np_hd_clinical_target_depth()`); ACC stays selectable and keeps the anxiety indication, but the spec states plainly that a 4×1 there is **indirect network modulation, not focal stimulation of the structure**, and callers must not present it as the latter. HD02-A is now checked in both directions — surface targets must be ≤35 mm, deep targets must be >35 mm — so reclassifying ACC to SURFACE to silence the test fails instead of passing vacuously (verified). **Deliberately NOT resolved by raising the 35 mm threshold**, which would have made the FAI assert focal reach the physics does not support and weakened the criterion for the six targets that pass with real margin. - **clinical-03 lateralization — option (b), narrow the zone (2026-07-20):** `clinical-03-pbm-cognitive-1064` retargeted from `Frontal Right` (11 sockets, includes midline 1/5/13) to a new `Frontal Right (excl. midline)` zone (8 sockets: 3, 6, 9, 10, 14, 15, 18, 19). - **Partial-module inclusion (option (c)) — deferral now evidence-backed, still deferred (2026-07-20):** NP-OPT-PSF-001 §4.1 models an aperture lighting only the ipsilateral half of a midline module: it still spills **22.1%** contralateral versus exactly 50% lit whole, i.e. it removes ~56% of the leak, while simply omitting the module takes it to zero. The mechanism is the 1064nm spatial resolution floor — an edge-spread function of **~26 mm** (10-90%) at cortical depth versus a 20 mm mask feature, so most of the masked half is re-illuminated by scatter from the lit half. Note the honest form of the argument: the optics does not prove masking useless, it proves masking **cannot reach zero** — and since zone narrowing reaches zero for free, that is enough to close the question. Partial-module inclusion must not be revisited as a lateralization tool; any future proposal needs a justification other than spatial selectivity at depth. Implementation still requires a fresh owner ruling.

  - **≥15 mm EEG separation is unachievable and is not claimed.** The retired 18–22 mm came from putting PBM FPCs and EEG cables on opposite sides of the shell wall. EEG electrodes now live *inside* T1-B tiles on the same L1 carrier as LED drive current; the best geometric separation left is the ~2–3 mm L1 laminate. Separation was always the *mechanism*; **<5 µVpp injected artifact is the requirement, and it is retained verbatim** (retired DRC-18c → SH2-DRC-16) and met by four mechanisms instead: shorten the analog path, DRL-driven guard plane, deterministic subtractable artifact, and loop-area minimization.
  - **The therapeutic band cannot be filtered off the bus — arithmetic, not opinion.** Holding a 40 Hz/25 % pulse at 0.25 A within 100 mV needs **15.6 mF per tile**; the PWM carrier needs ~62 µF and the edges ~10 µF. So on-tile decoupling kills the carrier and the edges and cannot touch the 0.5–100 Hz envelope, which *is* the therapy and sits in exactly the EEG band and the ELF band the Helmholtz loop cancels. It must be handled geometrically (broadside `VLED+`/`PGND` pairs, ≤25 mm² loop area per cluster feed — ~37 nT at 30 mm vs ~1.9 µT for a 5 mm-spaced pair) and computationally, never by filtering.
  - **Three prohibitions locked.** (1) The shell, shield stack, mu-metal and any DRL-driven structure **may not** carry LED or tES return current — the shell is a *driven* EEG shield (CLAUDE.md §4.3), so returning therapeutic-band current through it injects that current into the EEG reference. (2) N1 **may not** form a closed ring on L1 — a bowl-scale loop antenna and ground loop. (3) Spread-spectrum/dithered PWM is **prohibited**, inverting standard EMC practice: dither smears bus energy into broadband noise across the EEG band that neither the notch nor subtraction can remove, whereas a deterministic phase-locked artifact is a known, subtractable line.
  - **One aperture, segregated returns.** The interconnect shares the existing posterior-centre blind-mate boss (no second aperture to defend against the §5.3a λ/20 slot criterion), but the boss must present segregated contact groups with **independent returns** — the Helmholtz coils are the actuator of a loop whose fluxgate sensors sit on L1, and a shared return impedance would let the actuator modulate its own sensor's reference, nulling at the fluxgate while the field at the brain diverges (same failure shape as FMEA-G07-01: sensor reads nominal while the hazard grows). **Time-boxed: cheap now, expensive once MECH-1 tools the boss (OI-SHELL2-02).**
  - **Cancellation calibration is now configuration-dependent.** Because any tile type may occupy any socket, bus current distribution — and therefore self-field at each fluxgate — changes per configuration, so NP-HEX-ZM-001 §5.3.1 reason 4's "fixed coil-drive→field transfer" no longer holds unconditionally. Recalibration fires on `np_module_map` rebuild. Feed-forward self-field subtraction must bind to **measured PD/dose telemetry, not commanded current**, because LED aging, PD-metered compensation and the 62 °C throttle all make actual current diverge from commanded.

- **★ SYM-1 — the cluster partition is mirror-symmetric about the sagittal midline (principal direction,
