- **ACC reclassified as a DEEP (indirect-modulation) HD-tDCS target (NEW 2026-08-05)** — FAI HD02-A required all 7 clinical targets within 35 mm of their nearest cap electrode. ACC (0, 28, 28) is 47.1 mm from Fz and had been failing since the suite was written. It is not a mapping bug: ACC sits on the medial wall, its 2nd/3rd nearest electrodes are 49.1 mm, and no 10-10 scalp position gets closer than ~37.9 mm (Fpz) — the criterion is unsatisfiable for ACC by **any** electrode placement. The six other targets are cortical-surface at 11–29 mm. Root cause was a conflation in `np_hd_clinical_target_t` between *sLORETA source-localization* targets (deep sources localize fine) and *4×1 stimulation* targets (§2.3 claims ~1.5 cm FWHM at 10 mm depth). Resolution: targets now carry `NP_HD_TARGET_DEPTH_SURFACE`/`_DEEP` (`np_hd_clinical_target_depth()`); ACC stays selectable and keeps the anxiety indication, but the spec states plainly that a 4×1 there is **indirect network modulation, not focal stimulation of the structure**, and callers must not present it as the latter. HD02-A is now checked in both directions — surface targets must be ≤35 mm, deep targets must be >35 mm — so reclassifying ACC to SURFACE to silence the test fails instead of passing vacuously (verified). **Deliberately NOT resolved by raising the 35 mm threshold**, which would have made the FAI assert focal reach the physics does not support and weakened the criterion for the six targets that pass with real margin. See NP-FW-HD-001 §2.3, §4.3, §12. **Open:** app-side disclosure — the UI must surface the DEEP classification when a clinician selects ACC; the firmware exposes the class but nothing consumes it yet.
- **T2 clinical tACS driver: 21 channels, one per cap electrode (NEW 2026-08-05, supersedes "16-ch arbitrary waveform")** — the 16-channel figure in CLAUDE.md §3 T2 had no sourcing document, no BOM line, and no named silicon; `NP_HD_DRIVER_CHANNELS` was defined and never referenced. The wiring spec that would have pinned it (NP-HW-TCAP-001) does not exist, so `k_driver_channel[]` was a placeholder that wrapped electrodes 16–20 back onto channels 11–15. That wrap aliased geometric *neighbours* (Cz↔Pz, C4↔P4, T8↔P8, P7↔O1, P3↔O2), and since a 4×1 ring draws its cathodes from the electrodes nearest its anode, it guaranteed collisions — C4↔P4 made the M1_R ring undeliverable. 16 was never the binding number: a bilateral 4×1 energises 10 electrodes, so 16 channels were always sufficient; every collision came from the mapping, not the count. - **clinical-03 lateralization — option (b), narrow the zone (2026-07-20):** `clinical-03-pbm-cognitive-1064` retargeted from `Frontal Right` (11 sockets, includes midline 1/5/13) to a new `Frontal Right (excl. midline)` zone (8 sockets: 3, 6, 9, 10, 14, 15, 18, 19). Rationale: the protocol's entire Grade-A evidence base (Gonzalez-Lima / Barrett) is right-lateralized, and Monte Carlo optical modelling (NP-OPT-PSF-001) shows the inclusive zone puts **16.3% of its cortical energy contralateral**, falling to **3.6%** when narrowed — a 78% reduction (74-80% across the model's full sensitivity sweep). Cost: one zone definition, zero firmware. This is exactly the mechanism the PR #210 membership ruling designates (dis-inclusion = author a narrower zone; there is no runtime exclusion filter), and it depends on nothing gated by REG-1, since midline status derives from the validated socket row structure rather than from 10-20 registration. Option (a) accept-the-spill was rejected because the deviation is quantified, avoidable, and free to fix. Option (c) partial-module inclusion remains deferred and is now shown to be strictly dominated for this purpose — see below.
- **Partial-module inclusion (option (c)) — deferral now evidence-backed, still deferred (2026-07-20):** NP-OPT-PSF-001 §4.1 models an aperture lighting only the ipsilateral half of a midline module: it still spills **22.1%** contralateral versus exactly 50% lit whole, i.e. it removes ~56% of the leak, while simply omitting the module takes it to zero. The mechanism is the 1064nm spatial resolution floor — an edge-spread function of **~26 mm** (10-90%) at cortical depth versus a 20 mm mask feature, so most of the masked half is re-illuminated by scatter from the lit half. Note the honest form of the argument: the optics does not prove masking useless, it proves masking **cannot reach zero** — and since zone narrowing reaches zero for free, that is enough to close the question. Partial-module inclusion must not be revisited as a lateralization tool; any future proposal needs a justification other than spatial selectivity at depth. Implementation still requires a fresh owner ruling.

Monolithic L1 backplane: makes the socket layer a single non-repairable part, fights the compound curvature, and maximizes conductive mass on the one layer NP-HELMET-GEOM-001 §3.2 constrains to non-magnetic/low-eddy because the fluxgates mount there — rejected.
  - **Selected: cluster carriers.** The 3–7-tile cluster (**now 3–6 under CONTIG-1**; the 3-hex triad this range originally spanned is excluded by CLUSTER-1) that NP-HEX-ZM-001 §5.4a invented for a purely *mechanical* reason (one clamp actuator instead of 42 levers) is also the correct *electrical* aggregation boundary, for an independent reason: high-impedance analog (PD1/PD2 at 14–72 µA, EEG at sub-µV) cannot travel, and the cluster is the nearest conversion point that already exists. Five networks split by physics — N1 power, N2 control, N3 local sense (**≤50 mm, never leaves its carrier**), N4 electrode, N5 safety.

Consequence: **the module interconnect has zero dynamic-flex paths** — the retired architecture's dominant failure mode (fatigue cracking at a stiffener edge over 1,000 swap cycles) is designed out rather than mitigated. Non-obvious coupling recorded: contact count drives clamp-plate load (~38–63 N est. at 18 contacts per 7-tile cluster), so **contact count is a RISK-22 accessibility variable**, closed jointly with MECH-2 (OI-SHELL2-03).
  - **Safety: hardware enable per cluster, software enable per socket.** **⚠ Contested — NP-HW-HUB-001 Rev C §7.2 instead specifies a single `NP_SAFETY_EN_PBM_CRANIAL` bit; see the note at the end of this entry.** 80 sockets cannot each own a Safety MCU GPIO, and routing enable authority over I2C would put the main processor in the path — destroying the CLAUDE.md §4.2 property. `SAFE_EN_n` gates each cluster's high-side load switch, so no module firmware state, stuck I2C transaction or main-processor hang can produce emission from a de-energized cluster, and the <50 ms watchdog cutoff stays a rail cut rather than a message. Granularity **improves** 5 → ~12. This is the hardware half of OI-HUB-SOCKET-01; the socket-indexed control registry remains open there.
  - **≥15 mm EEG separation is unachievable and is not claimed.** The retired 18–22 mm came from putting PBM FPCs and EEG cables on opposite sides of the shell wall. EEG electrodes now live *inside* T1-B tiles on the same L1 carrier as LED drive current; the best geometric separation left is the ~2–3 mm L1 laminate. Separation was always the *mechanism*; **<5 µVpp injected artifact is the requirement, and it is retained verbatim** (retired DRC-18c → SH2-DRC-16) and met by four mechanisms instead: shorten the analog path, DRL-driven guard plane, deterministic subtractable artifact, and loop-area minimization.
  - **The therapeutic band cannot be filtered off the bus — arithmetic, not opinion.** Holding a 40 Hz/25 % pulse at 0.25 A within 100 mV needs **15.6 mF per tile**; the PWM carrier needs ~62 µF and the edges ~10 µF. So on-tile decoupling kills the carrier and the edges and cannot touch the 0.5–100 Hz envelope, which *is* the therapy and sits in exactly the EEG band and the ELF band the Helmholtz loop cancels. It must be handled geometrically (broadside `VLED+`/`PGND` pairs, ≤25 mm² loop area per cluster feed — ~37 nT at 30 mm vs ~1.9 µT for a 5 mm-spaced pair) and computationally, never by filtering.
  - **Three prohibitions locked.** (1) The shell, shield stack, mu-metal and any DRL-driven structure **may not** carry LED or tES return current — the shell is a *driven* EEG shield (CLAUDE.md §4.3), so returning therapeutic-band current through it injects that current into the EEG reference. (2) N1 **may not** form a closed ring on L1 — a bowl-scale loop antenna and ground loop. (3) Spread-spectrum/dithered PWM is **prohibited**, inverting standard EMC practice: dither smears bus energy into broadband noise across the EEG band that neither the notch nor subtraction can remove, whereas a deterministic phase-locked artifact is a known, subtractable line.
  - **One aperture, segregated returns.** The interconnect shares the existing posterior-centre blind-mate boss (no second aperture to defend against the §5.3a λ/20 slot criterion), but the boss must present segregated contact groups with **independent returns** — the Helmholtz coils are the actuator of a loop whose fluxgate sensors sit on L1, and a shared return impedance would let the actuator modulate its own sensor's reference, nulling at the fluxgate while the field at the brain diverges (same failure shape as FMEA-G07-01: sensor reads nominal while the hazard grows). **Time-boxed: cheap now, expensive once MECH-1 tools the boss (OI-SHELL2-02).**
  - **Cancellation calibration is now configuration-dependent.** Because any tile type may occupy any socket, bus current distribution — and therefore self-field at each fluxgate — changes per configuration, so NP-HEX-ZM-001 §5.3.1 reason 4's "fixed coil-drive→field transfer" no longer holds unconditionally. Recalibration fires on `np_module_map` rebuild. Feed-forward self-field subtraction must bind to **measured PD/dose telemetry, not commanded current**, because LED aging, PD-metered compensation and the 62 °C throttle all make actual current diverge from commanded.

- **Hub PCB Rev C — distributed cluster-controller tier replaces the 5-slot star (NP-HW-HUB-001 Rev C, 2026-07-29, DRAFT not yet baselined):** Closes the SMART-1 residual above. **Key finding: the binding constraint is interconnect, not I2C mux count.** Rev B is a star — every socket's analog *and* digital lines terminate at the hub — so scaling it to the 80-socket lattice needs ~11 conductors × 80 ≈ **880 conductors** through the single posterior blind-mate boss (NP-HEX-ZM-001 §5.3c), 160 TIA channels, 160 ADC channels against ~32 available on the RT1062, and 80 `GAIN_SEL` GPIO.
