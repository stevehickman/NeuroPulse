## LOG
text: **{date} — `OI-PRIV-32` (NP-PRIV-ANALYSIS-004) Q1 ANSWERED: the firmware rollback floor is stored in the eMMC's RPMB, with a NeurOne registry of serial number and minimum version as an optional, raise-only restore tool** (the principal, accepting the recommendation of `docs/human-input/oi-priv-32-firmware-rollback-floor-and-dfu.md` §4 and §4.1). RPMB allows a raise at every release and survives a factory reset; the registry restores a floor lost with a replaced or blank eMMC. Conditions: a blank or wrong-key RPMB refuses every image, as an unreadable floor already does (`REQ-SW02-15` (b)); the registry only ever raises a floor, its messages are signed, bound to one helmet's serial and encrypted in transit (`CLAUDE.md` §23); and no helmet ever needs it to install an image (`CLAUDE.md` §1, offline-capable). The registry adds availability, not security. Its data, firmware version against a device serial, is already SHDR, and it holds it only where the warranty owner consented (`REQ-SHDR-08`). Not built: the RPMB driver, the key's provisioning, the registry. Open: Q2 (every release or a rollback index) and Q3 (DFU encryption or a §23 exception). Owner: `docs/status/pending-decisions.md` `OI-PRIV-32` (NP-PRIV-ANALYSIS-004).
