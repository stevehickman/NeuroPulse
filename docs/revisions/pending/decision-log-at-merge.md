text: **Rev {CLAUDE.md to} ({date}) — footer and Document Map: a `completed-decisions.md` entry, this file's revision number and its history entry are written in the PR's revision fragment and applied when the PR merges. No locked decision changed.**
text: **What changed.** The footer said to log a changed decision in `docs/status/completed-decisions.md` and add an entry here, and every PR did both by hand, setting this file's `**Revision:**` and its row in the `NP-DHF-001` index as well. Now the decision-log entry goes in the `## LOG` section of the PR's revision fragment (`docs/revisions/pending/<slug>.md`) and the history entry in its `## CLAUDE.md` section, first paragraph `**Rev {CLAUDE.md to} ({date}) — `. The Document Map row for `docs/revisions/README.md` names both. `check-revision-fragments` rule K fails a PR that adds a log entry by hand, and rule L one that changes this file without a `## CLAUDE.md` section or sets any of the three numbers by hand. In mode `hand` authors do all of it by hand as before.
text: **Why.** The log is append-only with every entry at its end, and this history is newest-first with every entry at the top of "Current revision", so any two PRs in flight changed the same lines, and the second to merge conflicted there; two that changed this file also took the same revision number. That is the collision that moved revision numbers to merge time on 2026-11-02. With `main` now requiring a branch to be up to date before it merges (`OI-SWCI-53`), each merge sent every other such PR back to resolve it.
text: **What it leaves.** `docs/status/pending-decisions.md` is edited in place, not appended, and is not changed here. This revision is the first numbered by the workflow. The §1 to §6 and §16 to §23 numbering and every subsection number are unchanged.
