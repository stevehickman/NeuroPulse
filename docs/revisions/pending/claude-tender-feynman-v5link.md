## CLAUDE.md
text: **Rev {CLAUDE.md to} ({date}) — §4.2: until the photoparoxysmal detector is built, the goggles refuse photic driving at 3–60 Hz, not 3–30 Hz, so the 40 Hz gamma protocols are refused too (the principal, `OI-FWHUB-37`). No other locked decision changed.**
text: **What changed.** The EEG + Visual interlock row of §4.2 says the goggles refuse photic driving at 3–60 Hz outright, the 40 Hz gamma protocols included, until the Oz detector is built (`REQ-FWHUB-65`, `-66`). The firmware, the shared constants, the validator, the apps' strings and the tests carry the same edge.
text: **Why.** The 30 Hz edge had no source (`UC-121`). The photosensitivity literature puts the range at up to 60 Hz: the European photic-stimulation method tests to 60 Hz (Kasteleijn-Nolst Trenité et al., *Epilepsia* 2012;53:16-24), and about 49 % of photosensitive patients respond at 50 Hz and 15 % at 60 Hz (Fisher et al., *Epilepsia* 2005;46:1426-41). Cognito's HOPE Phase 3 trial of 40 Hz light and sound added a weak signal: seizures in 0.9 % of the active arm vs 0.3 % on sham. The principal chose to widen the zone over keeping 30 Hz with screening and a warning, because most photosensitive epilepsy is undiagnosed until a first seizure. The citations are checked by `OI-FWHUB-38`.
