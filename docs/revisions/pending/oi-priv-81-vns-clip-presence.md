text: - [ ] **`OI-PRIV-81` (raised 2026-11-26, found while correcting the hub's `contact_confirmed` comments after `OI-PRIV-40` (NP-PRIV-ANALYSIS-004), checked against `main`; `CLAUDE.md` §1, §4.2, §5.1; `data-architecture-detail.md` §5.1 (the in-session impedance row and the per-module running totals row); `REQ-FWHUB-93`, `REQ-VNSC-12`, `REQ-UPG-03`; `firmware/hub_control/modules/np_mod_vns.c` `np_mod_vns_detect()`, `firmware/hub_control/src/np_session_runner.c` `mark_fitted_modules()`, `firmware/hub_control/include/np_module_usage.h`) — the auricular VNS clip is detected by its contact impedance through the wearer, and that presence reaches SHDR in the per-module running totals.** `np_mod_vns_detect()` registers the clip only when `np_mod_vns_hal_impedance_check()` reads inside `[500 Ω, 5000 Ω]`, a window the clip reaches only through a person: an unworn clip reads open, as an unplugged one does. `mark_fitted_modules()` then marks the VNS port present, and that port's `sessions_present` and `present_s` are SHDR (`REQ-FWHUB-93`, the principal, 2026-11-24), uploaded in the monthly snapshot. So for this one port "present" means "worn when the hub scanned", a value derived from the in-session impedance check, which `OI-PRIV-40` rules UHDR and which no SHDR record may disclose. The two rulings are a day apart, and the 2026-11-24 one does not say how the clip's presence is sensed. Nothing else derived from the hub's VNS impedance read reaches SHDR: the reading and `contact_confirmed` stay in telemetry and UHDR; a refused contact sets the abort reason (UHDR) and the cause-free `stopped_early` bit that every stop sets (`OI-PRIV-22`); and the port's use totals follow an accepted command, while what was commanded is UHDR, so they do not tell a failed check from no command. **The fix is a presence signal a wearer cannot produce, sensed at the port** (what `OI-FWHUB-36` already asks for). The clip cable has no spare conductor (`REQ-VNSC-12`), so the signal comes from the connector or from circuits already in the clip, and the connector is unselected (`OI-VNSCLIP-06`, to be decided once across A11 to A14). Candidates: (a) a loop-back pin pair shorted inside the hub-side plug: no cable conductor, but it shows a seated plug, not a clip, which is enough only if clip and cable are one assembly; (b) a magnet in the plug and a Hall element at the port, as the goggles are detected (`np_mod_visual_hal_goggle_seated()`), or a receptacle with a built-in detect switch; (c) an ID or authentication chip in the hub-side plug, which also gives the clip a part identity and so answers the accessory-identity `OI-FWHUB-38` (`NP-FW-HUB-001`) for this accessory, as the intranasal probe and the cervical VNS accessory already authenticate; (d) with no hardware change, an electrical continuity test of the clip's PPG emitter at a current that does not light it, never using the photodiode, whose output depends on skin and placement (no optical ceiling is set for the ear, `OI-VNSCLIP-05`, and the PPG front end is unselected). **Any option keeps three things:** the contact impedance check stays as the contact-confirmation interlock (`CLAUDE.md` §4.2) and its result stays UHDR, so only its use as the presence input goes; a change to the accessory port lands on both tiers or neither (`REQ-UPG-03`); and "present" then means plugged in, not worn, which is what makes it SHDR and also makes it a weaker proxy for the skin and sweat exposure the 2026-11-24 ruling counts. **Until then** the VNS port's presence totals carry a wearer-derived value. Not counting that port's presence meanwhile moves data only toward UHDR, but departs from the 2026-11-24 ruling that every modality contributes, so it is the principal's call. Owner: EE + ME (the port signal, with `OI-VNSCLIP-06`), Firmware (`np_mod_vns_detect()` and the totals), the principal (the interim).
