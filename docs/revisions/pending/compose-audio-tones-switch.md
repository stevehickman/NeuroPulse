## NP-SW-001
author: NeurOne Software Engineering
text: **§6.3.8: SWR-UI-01 (f) status: the Compose audio editor no longer turns a typed binaural or isochronic rate of 0 into none. Each tone is present or absent by a switch, as on iOS, and its rate is a typed field. The exception for the binaural and isochronic zero-to-none conversion is removed from the status.**
