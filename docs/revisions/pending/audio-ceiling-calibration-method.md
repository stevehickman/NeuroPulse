## NP-HW-AUDIO-001
author: NeurOne Systems Engineering
text: **§5.1, §5.3, §9: OI-AUDIOHW-11 part decided. New §5.3 and REQ-AUDIO-22 to -25: the ceiling is verified at an ear simulator per unit and cup, never at the in-cup microphone (no cup-to-eardrum correction); the command ceiling is 85 minus a guard band of named terms whose sizes are open (UC-133); the calibration table is SHDR-class, bound, fail-closed and factory-written; A-weighting is computed from the IEC 61672-1 formula (-4.10 dB at 440 Hz, +1.27 dB at about 2.5 kHz) and every tone frequency is bounded at 1,000 Hz, awaiting the principal. Measurement on a built unit and the guard-band number stay open; UC-133 and UC-134 added.**
