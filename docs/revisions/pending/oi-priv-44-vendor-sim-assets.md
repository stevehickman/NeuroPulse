Each viewer's network address, and with no `Referrer-Policy` the page's URL, reach Google; on a marketing site (HOWTO §3 Option B, an `<iframe>`) that happens without consent, which the cited court held unlawful for Google Fonts. The importmap entries carry no integrity metadata, and Three.js runs with the page's full access, `window.__sim` (`OI-PRIV-45`) and the WebSocket included. HOWTO §2 documents the CDN load, not its privacy effect. **Repair proposed:** vendor the fonts and Three.js (the container of D-2, `docs/np_sim_fw_001.md` §9, is the natural place), add a CSP and `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`. **Closes when:** the page loads nothing from a third party. **Closed 2026-11-26 (done as proposed).** Three.js 0.164.1 with OrbitControls (from the npm tarball) and Inter 4.001 and JetBrains Mono 2.211 (the woff2 subsets Google served) are committed in `simulator/vendor/`, with each file's SHA-256 in `simulator/vendor/VENDOR.md`, and the page names only them; nothing builds or fetches them, and the D-2 container (`NP-SIM-FW-001` §9, checked: it assumed no CDN) needs no network for the page. `index.html` carries a CSP (`default-src 'none'`; scripts, styles and fonts from `'self'`, the importmap by its hash, `'wasm-unsafe-eval'` for the NPPS core; `connect-src 'self' ws://localhost:9000`) and `no-referrer`, as `<meta>` elements; the page's five `style` attributes became classes or CSSOM writes so the CSP refuses nothing it needs. `simulator/js/page-policy.test.mjs` tests the four parts of `REQ-SIMFW-30`; a Chromium load made no request off localhost and reported no CSP refusal. HOWTO §2 and §3 Option B say so. The licences are MIT and OFL 1.1, neither copyleft-obliging (`NP-SOUP-SIM-001`). **Not done here:** `frame-ancestors` cannot be set from a `<meta>`, so who may frame the page is left to the server (HOWTO §3); the CSP is not what keeps `window.__sim` from the page's own scripts (`OI-PRIV-45`, `REQ-SIMFW-27`) or other pages from the WebSocket (`OI-SIMFW-07`, `REQ-SIMFW-28`).
