# Photobiomodulation Protocols for Human Neurological Conditions

**Source:** Heiskanen PBM database (V. Heiskanen, www.bitly.com/PBM-database), Brain (672), Nervous system (301), Carpal tunnel (50) and Spinal column (19) categories. Column V legend: green ☺ = positive, yellow = unclear/modest, red ☹ = negative/null.
**Method:** Human trials extracted with full dosimetry (λ, mode, power, irradiance, fluence, time, sessions) and outcome. Parameters that converge across the *higher-quality* studies (randomized, sham-controlled, blinded, adequately dosed) are weighted above open-label/case reports. Where the evidence is only preclinical, a protocol is *projected* and flagged.
**Author:** compiled 2026-07-02.

## Evidence-grade key

- **A** — Multiple randomized sham-controlled trials, consistent direction, dose understood.
- **B** — ≥1 positive RCT plus supporting open-label; some negative trials attributable to under-dosing.
- **C** — Open-label / case series positive; RCTs absent, mixed, or under-powered.
- **D** — No human trial; projection from mechanism + animal data.

## The dosimetry lesson the database teaches

Three quality signals recur and drive every protocol below:

1. **A minimum cortical dose threshold exists.** The large *negative* stroke trials (NEST-1/2/3, 808 nm) and the negative depression trial (Iosifescu 2022, 830 nm) delivered too little energy to cortex per session, or only one session. The *positive* trials cluster at ≥ ~10–60 J/cm² measured *at the scalp* delivered *repeatedly*. Under-dosing, not mechanism failure, explains most nulls.
2. **NIR (800–1080 nm) penetrates; deep red (630–670 nm) mostly acts at scalp/systemically or intranasally.** Transcranial cortical targets use 810/1064 nm; 660 nm is an adjunct or intranasal/vascular channel.
3. **Monochromatic 890 nm LED pads ("MIRE"/Anodyne) repeatedly fail** for diabetic neuropathy in good RCTs — a specific, well-documented negative that should not be repeated.

NeurOne-relevant note: the device's native 660 + 810 + 1064 nm stack, 40 Hz pulsing, transcranial + intranasal channels, and EEG-adaptive closed loop map directly onto the strongest protocols below (Alzheimer's 40 Hz, cognitive 1064 nm PFC, depression bilateral DLPFC).

## DETAILED PROTOCOLS

### 1. Alzheimer's disease & dementia — Grade A
**Evidence:** 34 human studies, several double-blind RCTs positive. Woźniak-Mitał 2026 (n=33, DB): 850 nm, PW 40 Hz, 150 mW, 20 min, 50 sessions/10 wk → MMSE 21.9→27.2, ADAS-Cog 34.8→18.2 (p<0.0001). Chun 2026 (n=26, DB): 808 nm, 0.3 W/cm², 4400 J, 11 min, 72 sessions/12 wk → MoCA +3.87 vs −0.74 (p<0.001). Qi/Nizamutdinov 2021 (n=60, DB): 1060–1080 nm, 6 min twice-daily, 108 sessions/56 d → MMSE +4.8. Chao 2019 (DB): 810 nm 40 Hz, ↑perfusion + connectivity. Negative outlier: Chen 2023 (mixed) — still improved MMSE/ADL.

**Protocol:**
- **Wavelength:** 810 nm (transcranial) **and/or** 1060–1080 nm; add 660 nm intranasal.
- **Mode/frequency:** Pulsed **40 Hz** (gamma-entrainment rationale, Tsai/GENUS) is the emerging preferred mode; CW is acceptable at 1060 nm.
- **Irradiance:** 0.1–0.3 W/cm² at scalp (≤ ~2% reaches cortex).
- **Dose:** 20–75 J/cm² per site per session.
- **Duration:** 6 min (high-power 1060 nm) to 20–25 min (810 nm).
- **Sites:** whole-head (bilateral frontal, temporal, parietal + midline) + bilateral intranasal.
- **Schedule:** 3–7×/week, minimum 8–12 weeks; benefit accrues and partially sustains at follow-up. Maintenance dosing indicated.

### 2. Mild cognitive impairment (MCI) — Grade A
**Evidence:** Rashidi-Ranjbar 2026 (DB, sham): 810 nm PW, 20 min → global cognition d=1.05, episodic memory d=1.09, ↑DMN connectivity, ↓lactate/pyruvate ratio. Papi 2022 (sham): 850 nm, 400 mW, 0.285 W/cm², 10 min, 5 sessions → MMSE 18→27. de Oliveira 2024 (n=93, sham): MoCA +3.2 vs +1.97 (p=0.03), ↑BDNF, persisted 3 mo. Lee 2025, Chan 2021 corroborate.
**Protocol:** 810 nm (or 850 nm) PW 40 Hz or CW; 0.02–0.3 W/cm²; 7–60 J/cm²; 6–20 min; prefrontal (F3/F4) ± whole-head; 3–5×/week, 6–12 weeks.

### 3. Cognitive enhancement — healthy adults & cognitive aging — Grade A
**Evidence:** The most replicated acute effect in the database. Gonzalez-Lima/Barrett lineage — 1064 nm, 0.25 W/cm², 60 J/cm²/site, 8 min, right (or bilateral) PFC: Barrett & Gonzalez-Lima 2013, Blanco 2016/2017, Hwang 2016, Vargas 2017, Holmes 2019, Zhao 2022, Qu 2022, Yang 2024/2025 — consistent gains in attention (PVT), working memory (n-back, delayed-match-to-sample), executive/set-shifting, with fNIRS-verified ↑prefrontal oxygenation. Repeated dosing (7 days, Qu 2022) outperforms single and lasts ≥3 weeks.
**Protocol:**
- **Wavelength:** 1064 nm (CW). *This is the gold-standard acute cognitive parameter set.*
- **Irradiance:** 0.25 W/cm²; **Dose:** 60 J/cm² per site (≈816–1632 J total over 1–2 sites).
- **Duration:** 8 min (240 s/site × 2 sites, or 480 s single site).
- **Site:** right lateral PFC (Fp2/F4 region); bilateral for broader effect.
- **Schedule:** single session for acute boost; daily/near-daily ×1 week for durable multi-week gains.

### 4. Depression (major depressive disorder) — Grade B
**Evidence:** Cassano 2018 (DB RCT, n=21): 823 nm CW, 36 mW/cm², up to 65 J/cm², 20–30 min, 16 sessions/8 wk → HAM-D effect size 0.9–1.5. Schiffer 2009 (crossover): 810 nm, 250 mW/cm², 60 J/cm², 4 min → remission 6/10 at 2 wk. Disner 2016 (sham RCT): 1064 nm right forehead, benefit contingent on attention bias. Barrett 2025 (sham RCT, n=60): 1064 nm adjunct → +30% symptom reduction over sham. Henderson & Morries 2017 (retrospective): high-power 810/980 nm → robust response in 36/39. **Quality nuance:** Iosifescu 2022 (n=54, DB) *negative* at 830 nm — attributable to sub-threshold dose; a minimum dose threshold is explicitly invoked by the authors.
**Protocol:**
- **Wavelength:** 810–830 nm bilateral DLPFC; optionally 1064 nm to right PFC.
- **Mode:** CW or 40 Hz.
- **Irradiance:** 0.03–0.25 W/cm²; **Dose target:** ≥60 J/cm² per site (do not under-dose).
- **Duration:** 8–30 min; **Sites:** F3 and F4 (bilateral DLPFC).
- **Schedule:** 2–3×/week, 6–8 weeks; long-term augmentation tolerated.

### 5. Anxiety / GAD — Grade B
**Evidence:** Maiello 2019 (open, n=15): 830 nm, 30 mW/cm², 36 J/cm², 20 min daily 8 wk → SIGH-A 17.3→8.5 (d=1.47). Wang 2023 (DB RCT, n=36): 820 nm, 0.31 W/cm², forehead → HAMA reduced vs sham (p=0.021), sustained 8 wk. Right-PFC 1064 nm (Barrett lineage) also anxiolytic.
**Protocol:** 820–830 nm (or right-PFC 1064 nm); CW or 10 Hz; 0.03–0.31 W/cm²; 10–36 J/cm²; 10–20 min; right or bilateral PFC; daily, 8 weeks.

### 6. Traumatic brain injury (chronic) & post-concussion syndrome — Grade B
**Evidence:** Best for *chronic* TBI. Naeser 2011/2014 (case series/single-arm): 633+870 nm CW, 22 mW/cm², 13 J/cm², transcranial + intranasal, 3×/wk → executive-function gains. Longo 2020 (DB RCT, n=68): 810 nm → significant DTI (white-matter) changes at 3 mo, symptom trend. Rindner 2022: 1064 nm, 0.25 W/cm², 120 J/cm², 20 min, 8 wk → 9/11 clinically improved. Hipskind 2018, Chao 2020: 810/850 nm pulsed 10/40 Hz, ↑rCBF. **Quality nuance:** Taylor 2023 (sham RCT) *negative* for persistent post-concussion — acute/sub-acute concussion responds less than chronic TBI.
**Protocol:** 810 nm (± 633 nm adjunct); PW 10–40 Hz or CW; 0.02–0.25 W/cm²; 10–120 J/cm²; 10–20 min; multi-site scalp (frontal/temporal/parietal, target injury) + bilateral intranasal; 3×/week ≥6 weeks. Chronic > acute.

### 7. Autism spectrum disorder (pediatric) — Grade B
**Evidence:** Fradkin 2024 (DB RCT, n=30): 850 nm PW **40 Hz**, ≤300 mW, 5–6 min, 16 sessions/8 wk → CARS between-group Δ 7.23 (p=0.011), EEG delta/theta changes. Fradkin 2025 (n=23): CARS −7 (p<0.0001), ↑gamma/beta. Ceranoglu 2022, Pallanti 2022 (810 nm) support; Leisman 2018 (635 nm) positive on irritability.
**Protocol:** 850 nm, **PW 40 Hz**; low power (≤300 mW total, ~150 mW/emitter); short exposure 5–12 min (start 2 min, titrate up for tolerability); temporal + posterior/base-of-skull; 2×/week, 8 weeks. Pediatric — conservative dosing and tolerability titration essential.

### 8. Parkinson's disease — Grade C
**Evidence:** Open-label/case series consistently positive; RCTs confounded by strong placebo response. Liebert 2021/2024 (waitlist-controlled + follow-up): 904 nm laser + 810 nm LED, transcranial **+ neck + abdomen/gut** → mobility, cognition, dynamic balance, fine motor, smell improved, many gains sustained ≥1 yr. Hong 2021 (single-arm): 940 nm, 30 min daily 2 wk (+H₂) → UPDRS fell from week 1. Bicknell 2026 (5-yr case series): 904 nm → 5/6 no motor decline over 5 yr, gut-microbiome shifts. Santos 2019 (sham RCT): 670 nm. McGee 2023 / Herkes 2023: 635+810 nm mixed on total UPDRS but sub-scores improved.
**Protocol (rationale: gut-brain + transcranial):**
- **Wavelength:** 810 nm + 634/660 nm transcranial; 904 nm laser to scalp; consider 1060 nm.
- **Sites:** head (bilateral, midline over SMA/motor) **plus neck (vagus) and abdomen (enteric/microbiome)** — the combination is the signal.
- **Mode:** PW (10–40 Hz) transcranial; **Duration:** 15–30 min; **Schedule:** daily or 6×/week tapering, ≥12 weeks then home maintenance.
- Targets both motor and non-motor (cognition, balance, smell, sleep) domains.

### 9. Stroke — Grade C (chronic rehab) / **Avoid acute**
**Evidence — acute (negative, high quality):** NEST-1 (Lampl 2007) positive but NEST-2 (Zivin 2009) and NEST-3 (Hacke 2014, n=566, terminated for futility) — single 808 nm treatment within 24 h **failed**; insufficient cortical dose suspected. **Do not use PBM as acute stroke monotherapy.**
**Evidence — chronic/rehab (positive):** Naeser 2019 (case series): 633+870 nm, ipsilesional scalp → aphasia/naming + fMRI gains. Estrada-Rojas 2023: 630+660+850 nm + speech therapy → marked dysarthria improvement. Huang 2025 (RCT, n=90): 630 nm phototherapy → post-stroke cognition, mood, ADL. ILIB (Lai 2022) → better mRS.
**Protocol (chronic rehab adjunct):** 810 nm (+ 633 nm) transcranial to perilesional cortex + midline, + intranasal, ~0.02 W/cm², ~13 J/cm², 10–20 min, 3×/week, **paired with physical/speech therapy**. Post-stroke cognitive impairment: add 630–810 nm regimen.

### 10. Bell's palsy / peripheral facial nerve palsy — Grade B
**Evidence:** 21 human studies, largely positive. Standard: 830 nm, 100 mW, ~10 J/cm²/point along facial nerve + stylomastoid foramen, 3×/wk, 6 wk (Ordahan 2017, Yamada 1995, Murakami 1993). Alayat 2014 (RCT): 830 + 1064 nm HILT most effective. Wu 2023/2024 (RCT, n=84): 808+905 nm laser-acupuncture → House-Brackmann OR 0.11 (p<0.001). Pasquale 2021: 808 nm, 1 W/cm², 60 J/cm², recovered 11/14. **Best when treated early** (chronic >6 mo responds poorly).
**Protocol:** 808–830 nm; CW or PW; 100 mW–1 W; ~10 J/cm² per point (~30–60 s/point); points along facial nerve trunk, branches, and stylomastoid foramen; 12–18 sessions at 3×/week over ~6 weeks. Add 1064 nm HILT for stronger effect. Treat as early as possible.

### 11. Carpal tunnel syndrome / peripheral nerve entrapment — Grade B
**Evidence:** 38 human studies — net modestly positive, dose-dependent (many nulls trace to low dose or wrong parameters). Positive: Ekim 2007, Fusakul 2014, Lazovic 2014, Naeser 2002 (crossover, refractory cases → sustained 1–3 yr), Shooshtari 2008, Yagci 2009. Negative/null: Tascioglu 2012, Irvine 2004, Rayegani 2013 (under-dosed). Comparators show efficacy ≈ ultrasound/kinesiotape, below surgery.
**Protocol:** 780–830 nm (± 904 nm); CW or PW 10 Hz; over carpal tunnel/median nerve at ~5 points; **6–10 J/cm² per point** (this dose window separates positive from null trials); 10–15 sessions at 3–5×/week over 2–5 weeks. Best for mild-to-moderate CTS as conservative alternative.

### 12. Diabetic peripheral neuropathy — Grade C (honest mixed evidence)
**Evidence:** Genuinely split. **Negative (good RCTs):** Anodyne/MIRE 890 nm LED — Lavery 2008, Clifft 2005, Franzen-Körzendörfer 2008, Nawfar 2011 all null. **Positive:** Chatterjee 2019 (RCT): deep laser 810(20%)+980(80%) nm, 2 W, 13 J/cm² → pain + QoL improved. da Silva Leal 2020 (sham RCT): ILIB 660 nm → lower pain/better QoL. Oggiam 2024 (RCT, n=168): 890 nm → less pain, better sleep. 830/850 nm laser (Yamany 2012) improved microcirculation.
**Protocol (weighted to what worked):** Avoid low-power 890 nm LED pads as monotherapy. Use higher-irradiance **810+980 nm** or **830 nm** laser to feet/distal limbs, ~10 J/cm², 2–3×/week ≥4 weeks; consider **ILIB 660 nm** systemic channel. Set expectations: effect on pain/QoL more reliable than on nerve-conduction reversal.

### 13. Spinal cord injury — Grade C
**Evidence:** Liang 2026 (multicenter RCT, n=66): 810 nm, 300 mW, 30 min ×7 d via **surgically implanted scattering fiber** → ASIA motor +8.1 (0.5 mo) and +13.3 (3 mo), converging by 12 mo. Liang 2022 (feasibility): safe. da Silva 2018/2020 (sham RCTs): 808 nm transcutaneous + physiotherapy → better sensory-motor recovery + QoL. Huang 2012 (ILIB): mitochondrial/antioxidant markers up.
**Protocol:** 808–810 nm. *Transcutaneous* (non-invasive): 120–300 mW, high cumulative fluence, 30 min, daily → weeks, paired with physiotherapy. *Invasive* (research/clinical, acute post-decompression): implanted fiber 300 mW 30 min ×7 d. Penetration to cord is the limiting factor transcutaneously — maximize dose.

### 14. Multiple sclerosis — Grade C (symptomatic, not disease-modifying)
**Evidence:** Weak/peripheral. Rouhani 2024 (DB crossover, n=31): high-dose PBM to muscle → force recovery + strength improved (not CNS-targeted). Silva 2020: 808 nm → ↑IL-10. Fatigue outcomes null (Silva 2022). No transcranial CNS efficacy demonstrated.
**Protocol (symptom management only):** 808 nm to affected musculature for strength/fatigue-of-effort, higher dose (40–120 J), ~6 min/site. Do **not** claim disease modification. Transcranial MS protocol is unproven.

### 15. Epilepsy — Grade D (projected — CAUTION)
**Evidence:** 12 database entries, **0 human trials**; preclinical only (NIR transcranial anticonvulsant signals in animal models via mitochondrial/anti-inflammatory pathways).
**Projected protocol (low confidence):** 810 nm CW, *low* irradiance, focus-targeted transcranial, short sessions, under neurologist supervision with EEG monitoring.
**Critical safety caveat:** This applies only to **non-visual NIR transcranial PBM**. **Visual/photic flicker stimulation (any device flicker channel) is contraindicated / a seizure trigger** in photosensitive epilepsy — photoparoxysmal response must be excluded first. NeurOne's Oz photoparoxysmal detection + <200 ms goggle halt is the relevant safety interlock; the visual modality must be disabled for this population. No PBM epilepsy treatment claim is currently supportable in humans.

### 16. Emerging / thin-evidence conditions — Grade C/D
- **PTSD:** small case/RCT signal; 1070 nm PW 10 Hz and combined with TBI protocols (Lee 2025 crossover: ↓PTSD symptoms). Project: 810/1070 nm PFC, 40/10 Hz, as per depression/anxiety.
- **Addiction (opioid):** 9 entries, early; project PFC 810 nm 40 Hz.
- **ADHD:** 2 entries; project prefrontal 810 nm, per cognitive protocol, pediatric-conservative.
- **Bipolar / schizophrenia:** minimal (4/2 entries) — no protocol; caution (activation risk in bipolar).
- **Cerebral palsy, Down syndrome, Rett, cognitive/developmental:** isolated positive case series (e.g., Mannu 2019 Down syndrome, 830 nm 40 J/cm²); projectable from the general transcranial 810/830 nm framework, individualized, pediatric-conservative.

## Cross-cutting protocol principles (from the highest-quality studies)

1. **Hit the dose threshold, repeatedly.** Aim ≥10–60 J/cm² at scalp per site; single sessions rarely suffice for chronic CNS conditions — think 3–7×/week over 8–12+ weeks with maintenance.
2. **Match wavelength to depth.** 1064 nm and 810 nm for cortex; 660 nm intranasal/vascular/systemic; combine wavelengths for multi-depth coverage.
3. **40 Hz pulsing** is the emerging preferred mode for dementia, autism, and gamma-linked cognition; CW is standard for acute cognitive (1064 nm) and many peripheral protocols.
4. **Target the network, not one spot** — whole-head + intranasal for global cognition; bilateral DLPFC for mood; add neck/gut for Parkinson's.
5. **Respect the documented nulls** — no acute-stroke monotherapy, no low-power 890 nm MIRE pads for neuropathy, no under-dosed depression regimens, no photic flicker in photosensitive epilepsy.
6. **Pair with rehab/therapy** where the condition is recovery-based (stroke, SCI, Bell's palsy, MS strength).
